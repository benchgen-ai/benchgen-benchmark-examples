# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where you represent this project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback kindly, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to **hello@benchgen.com**. Reports are handled by the maintainers, and the reporter's identity is kept private.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious problems.
